Research

Western Himalaya · Kashmir

My work joins field investigations, laboratory hydraulics, HEC-HMS and HEC-RAS modelling, and AI-based forecasting. It is grounded in the rivers and glacial lakes of the Western Himalaya, where floods arrive quickly, gauges are sparse and many lakes cannot be reached on foot.

Doctoral thesis

Investigations of Flood Discharges in Mountainous Streams Using Statistical, Artificial Intelligence and Hydrological Techniques

Supervised by Dr Mahesh Patel, Department of Civil Engineering, NIT Jalandhar

Glacial lake outburst floods

A probabilistic framework for GLOF hazard at moraine-dammed lakes too remote to survey, and a closer look at what evidence early warning systems need to move from detection to action.

Discharge forecasting

Predicting monthly flow of the Sindh River with support vector regression, k-nearest neighbours and gradient boosting.

Steep-stream flow & bed morphology

Laboratory and field study of how flow and bed form interact in steep mountain streams, under an ANRF-funded project completed in November 2025.

Sediment transport

Bringing physics-based and AI-driven approaches together for sediment transport modelling.

Hydraulic instrumentation

Designing laboratory hardware for open-channel experiments: a real-time water surface profiler, a hydraulic lab trolley and a 3D sonic bed mapper.

Climate & hydropower

How a changing climate affects water availability and hydropower generation, the subject of my M.Tech thesis.
